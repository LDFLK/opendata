introduction: |
  Many government statutory bodies are required to meet by Acts of Parliament, some at set intervals. Yet there are no public records of whether they actually do.

  So the Lanka Data Foundation (LDF) asked them directly, under the Right to Information Act No. 12 of 2016.

  38 statutory bodies were selected across 17 Ministries. Each body was asked the same three questions: the dates of their last three meetings, the meeting minutes or a summary of the decisions made during those meetings, and the current membership of the meeting body.  

  Of the 38 selected bodies, 20 responded with the requested information, 3 acknowledged the request but failed to send the information  5 rejected the request, declining to provide the information and 10 did not respond at all.
